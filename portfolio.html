Product portfolio

What we build

Our current repository documents electronics product engineering around six core solution areas. The details below expand those documented areas without inventing specific product names, specifications or customer claims.

Product information: The repository currently provides product categories and engineering focus areas rather than individual commercial product datasheets. This page therefore presents clear product-family information based on the documented NK-TECHIES scope.

Hardware

Custom PCB Products

Custom electronic boards designed around a product's functional and integration requirements, from circuit concept through prototype-ready hardware.

Engineering scope

  • Schematic and hardware architecture
  • PCB layout and board-level integration
  • Interfaces for sensors and external devices
  • Prototype-focused hardware development

Best suited for

New electronic products, control boards, sensor interfaces and custom hardware prototypes.

Embedded

Embedded Controllers

Microcontroller-based product electronics that combine sensing, control logic, firmware and device-level operation.

Engineering scope

  • Microcontroller-based control systems
  • Firmware and device logic
  • Sensor and peripheral interfaces
  • Hardware and firmware integration

Best suited for

Smart devices, controllers, sensor-driven equipment and embedded product prototypes.

Connected

IoT & Connected Devices

Connected electronic products designed to collect information, communicate with software systems and support remote monitoring or intelligent operation.

Engineering scope

  • Connected device architecture
  • Telemetry and monitoring concepts
  • Device-to-software integration
  • Embedded connectivity workflows

Best suited for

Remote monitoring, connected equipment, telemetry and smart electronic products.

Automation

Industrial Automation Systems

Control and monitoring solutions for equipment and industrial environments, combining electronics, embedded control and supporting software.

Engineering scope

  • Equipment control concepts
  • Monitoring and sensor integration
  • Automation-oriented embedded systems
  • Hardware and software integration

Best suited for

Industrial equipment, control applications, monitoring systems and automation projects.

Product Platform

Connected Product Platforms

Integrated product architectures that bring electronics and software together around a complete connected-product experience.

Engineering scope

  • Hardware and software integration
  • Device-to-application workflows
  • Connected product architecture
  • Prototype-to-product engineering direction

Best suited for

Products that need coordinated hardware, embedded systems, connectivity and software.

Software

Device Software

Firmware and supporting software that make electronic products useful, controllable and integrated with their surrounding systems.

Engineering scope

  • Embedded firmware
  • Device control and logic
  • Supporting product software
  • Electronics and software integration

Best suited for

Embedded products, connected devices, controllers and electronics requiring software intelligence.

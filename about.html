About Us

Company History

NK-TECHIES is a Consulting company, established in the year 2017.
We provide Human Capital Solutions in IT field to wide range of Clients across Various Industries.

NK-TECHIES was established with a Mission to provide competent Human Resources to the IT sectors in a cost-effective manner.

We provide a wide range of services in Human Resource field i.e. Placement Services, Contract and Projects Staffing and Project Outsourcing.

Our years of experience & dedication have enabled us to use our wide & sophisticated network in IT Industry to identify & choose the best suitable candidates for each assignment.

Who we are

An engineering-focused technology company.

NK-TECHIES was established in 2017. The company has its roots in IT consulting and human capital solutions and is now positioned around a broader technology offering that combines electronics engineering with software development.

Our product-engineering direction covers custom electronics, PCB design, embedded systems, IoT connectivity, industrial automation and the software platforms that support connected products.

We aim to make the boundary between hardware and software simpler for customers by bringing the technical layers together around the product requirement.

Our focus

Four connected engineering layers.

Hardware

Electronics architecture, schematic thinking, PCB design and prototype support.

Embedded

Firmware, device interfaces, control logic and communications.

Connectivity

IoT, telemetry, remote monitoring and device-to-cloud integration.

Software

Web, mobile, APIs, dashboards and applications around the product.

Our history

Experience in people, technology and delivery.

NK-TECHIES was established with a mission to provide competent technology resources and practical solutions to businesses. Our existing IT consulting and staffing experience remains part of the company while the product-engineering focus expands into electronics and connected technology.
